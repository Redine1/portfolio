About My Journey & Vision

A passionate Computer Engineering student driven to solve real-world problems through embedded hardware and robust software systems.

I am Redine Kyle Villabito Elejorde, a 21-year-old Computer Engineering undergraduate at ICCT Colleges. My fascination with technology thrives in the space where code meets physical circuitry.

Rather than confining myself to only software or only electronics, I cultivate a dual passion: engineering low-level embedded hardware solutions—such as sensor-driven agricultural telemetry and custom microcontrollers—while simultaneously developing responsive full-stack web platforms, Android apps, and high-performance desktop applications.

From designing the NeuroComm Board and authoring my ongoing thesis on an automated Solar-Powered Subsurface Irrigation System, to building community alerting systems like FLOODGUARD, my work is centered on building purposeful technology with tangible impact.
